Scoring standards

Credibility is judged on checkable evidence for a claim: reviewed data or method, whether the cited study exists, its correction record, and independent corroboration. A source is not down-weighted for who published it or which group they belong to. A correct sub-claim keeps its support when another conclusion from the same source is refuted. Nothing is refuted without recorded counterevidence. EPIC does not censor.

Reference library

A matching entry is reused with its recorded evidence. Keyword and question text are both searched. A stale entry is not shown as the current answer. A different entered scope is a different question. Automated research of a new point is not implemented.

Verified knowledge claims

EPIC FABLE

Fictional Analysis Based on Lore Evidence. This is fiction. Lore is not evidence and is never part of the factual reference library.

An AI matchup run is not implemented. In a debate, humans argue from their own knowledge and must supply each power or feat and its canon citation. The referee rules only those moves. It does not coach, suggest powers, answer questions, or introduce a counter a debater did not raise. Automated AI refereeing is not implemented. A moderator can rule by hand. EPIC cannot fully prevent a debater from consulting material outside this room. Debate outcomes and AI matchup outcomes are stored separately.
