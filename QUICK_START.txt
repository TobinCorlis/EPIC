EPIC 1.0 RC1 — QUICK START

WINDOWS
1. Install Python 3.11 or newer from python.org.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Open the scripts folder.
4. Double-click start_epic_windows.bat.
5. When the browser opens, register your account.

MAC OR LINUX
1. Open Terminal in the extracted EPIC folder.
2. Run: ./scripts/start_epic.sh
3. Open: http://127.0.0.1:8000

YOUR LOCAL DATA
Your local debates and accounts are saved in backend/epic.db.
Keep that file to preserve your local EPIC data.

STOPPING EPIC
Return to the terminal window and press Ctrl+C.
